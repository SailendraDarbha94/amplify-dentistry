## How the research was produced

A multi-agent workflow ran ten research dimensions in parallel, each agent reading at least fifteen primary sources. Every risky or uncertain claim (263 of 272) was then handed to two independent fact-checkers told to refute it; corrected text replaced the original wherever a checker found an error. A completeness critic identified six gaps; four were researched inside the workflow and two (imaging reality, voice of Indian dentists) by separate agents. Four section writers and an editor produced the documents, and the editor's cross-section fixes were applied by hand.

## How to read the verification tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `confirmed` | passed two independent fact-checks |
| `corrected` | a checker corrected the claim; the text shown is the corrected version |
| `disputed` | the two checkers disagreed; treat as uncertain |
| `unverified`, `unverified-low-risk` | not fact-checked |
| `gap-fill-unverified` | from the gap-fill round; single pass, not checked |

Across the ten main dimensions: 82 confirmed, 157 corrected, 24 disputed, 9 unverified. The 58 gap-fill findings and the two extra reports are single-pass.

## Evidence limits

Reddit, DentalTown, G2 and most Capterra pages blocked the research tools, so Western dentist sentiment is relayed through secondary roundups; Indian sentiment comes from Google Play, App Store, Capterra India, Trustpilot and Quora directly. No published Indian source measures patients per day, receptionist presence or adult no-show rates for solo clinics; those must come from interviews and the Oasis Dental Clinic pilot.
