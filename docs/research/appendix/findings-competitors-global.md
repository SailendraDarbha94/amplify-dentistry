## Summary

The global dental practice-management software (PMS) market in 2025-26 is a USD 2-3.5B category growing 8-10%/yr, dominated by three ownership blocs: Henry Schein One (a Henry Schein + Internet Brands JV holding Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, Jarvis Analytics 80%, Lighthouse 360, Dentally, Software of Excellence EXACT, Elite Computer Italia), Patterson (Eaglesoft + Fuse, taken private by Patient Square Capital for USD 4.1B on 17 Apr 2025), and PE-backed Planet DDS (Denticon, Apteryx imaging, Cloud 9 ortho, 14,500 practices). Around them sit well-funded cloud challengers (Curve ~USD 100M ARR and claiming ~20% of cloud seats; CareStack, Indian-engineered from Trivandrum, 3,000+ practices; Oryx, dentist-founded), the open-database incumbent Open Dental (USD 199 then 149/location/month, free in developing countries), and a thick layer of bolt-ons for communications (Weave, NYSE: WEAV, ~USD 237M FY25 revenue guide; Adit; NexHealth), analytics (Dental Intelligence/Modento, Jarvis) and AI radiology (Overjet, valued USD 712M post Dec-2025; Pearl, USD 349/mo). Pricing is almost universally opaque and quote-based; real-world US prices run USD 150-1,400 per location per month, UK Dentally runs GBP 125-945/month per 3-surgery site, and the single most common review complaint across Denticon, NexHealth, tab32 and Cloud 9 is price creep and add-on fees after signing.

The product centre of gravity in every incumbent is the US insurance revenue cycle (Dentrix Ascend ran 191M eligibility checks and 22M digital forms in 2025; CareStack's ERA posting; Curve's claims attachments), desktop/browser charting, and now AI agents for voice notes, image QA and front-desk automation (Ascend Voice Notes on AWS, Planet DDS "AI-first" DentalOS, CareStack Connie/Colby agents, Oryx AI tier at USD 1,399/mo, Dentally AI notes with 45-90 transcriptions per surgery per month). What none of them has is a phone-first clinical app for the dentist: Dentrix Ascend's app is for VoIP calls, Curve Mobile is schedule/texting/photos with no charting, Open Dental's ODTouch is tablet-only (its chart screen is "not available when using a mobile phone device") and US-only at USD 85/mo, Dentally says "no app, just Chrome", and Dental Intelligence's iOS app sits at 1.6/5 stars. Clinical work on a phone, consent capture, lab-case and implant tracking, and cash-pay billing without CDT/ADA claim plumbing are all weakly served.

For a dental-only, India-first, globally credible product the whitespace is therefore: (1) a native Android/iOS app where the dentist can chart (tooth-level odontogram, perio, procedures), build and get e-signed treatment plans and procedure-specific consents, review images, prescribe and bill in under a minute between patients; (2) transparent, published, per-clinic pricing with no add-on ladder, which every review site says incumbents fail at; (3) a cash/UPI/GST billing core with insurance as an optional module, inverting the US-centric design; (4) offline-tolerant architecture, because the Change Healthcare outage (Feb-Apr 2024) and the Henry Schein breach (Oct 2023, 35 TB exfiltrated, 166,432 people notified) showed how brittle single-clearinghouse, single-vendor stacks are; and (5) open data export and partner hooks, since Pearl/Overjet-style AI radiology, communications and analytics vendors already integrate with 8+ PMSs and should be partners, not rebuilt. The incumbents' stated strategy (Planet DDS and Henry Schein One both lead with 100+ location DSOs; Ascend Accelerate is a DSO tier) leaves solo and 2-5 chair practices, which are the overwhelming majority in India and still 84% of US dentists, as the under-served segment.

Caveats: most price points are third-party listings, not vendor list prices (exceptions: Open Dental, Oryx and Dentally UK publish prices); circulating market-share percentages (Dentrix 18-22% etc.) have no traceable methodology, so installed-base claims from vendors are the only hard numbers; and my DentalTown/Reddit evidence is secondhand because the search budget ran out before I could open threads directly.

### 1. `corrected` · confidence medium

Global dental practice management software (PMS) market estimates for 2025 range from about USD 1.8B to 3.2B, with forecast CAGRs of roughly 8-11% (one as low as 7.9%): Research and Markets / The Business Research Company USD 1.77B (2025) to 1.91B (2026), 8.1% 2025-26 and 7.9% CAGR 2026-30 (Feb 2026 edition); Fortune Business Insights USD 1.96B (2025) to 2.15B (2026) and 4.87B (2034) at 10.77% CAGR 2026-34; Grand View Research USD 3.1B (2025) to 3.4B (2026) and 6.8B (2033) at 10.6% CAGR 2026-33 (its snapshot graphic also shows 10.9%, an internal inconsistency); Precedence Research USD 3.18B (2025) to 3.51B (2026) and 8.29B (2035) at 10.06% CAGR; Straits Research USD 3.02B (2025) to 3.31B (2026) and 6.95B (2034) at 9.7% CAGR. **Why it matters:** The TAM is real but mid-sized and slow-growing; a niche entrant wins on segment focus (India, solo/small practices, mobile) rather than on riding category growth. Use a range, not a single number, in any pitch.
