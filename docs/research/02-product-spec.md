## Product principles

1. **The dentist's phone is the primary clinical surface.** Chart, consent, prescribe, bill and book the next sitting in under 60 seconds between patients, one-handed. No incumbent does this: Open Dental's ODTouch chart is "not available when using a mobile phone device" (https://www.opendental.com/manual/odtouchchart.html), Curve Mobile lists schedule, texting and photos but no charting (https://www.curvedental.com/mobile), Dentee's iOS app sits at 1.7/5 from 14 ratings (https://apps.apple.com/in/app/dentee-for-doctors/id1081805174), and a 7-year Practo Ray user reports the plan can mark a whole tooth but not surfaces (https://yourdentalreview.com/practo-patient-management-software-ray/).
2. **The competitor is paper plus personal WhatsApp, not Dentrix.** A 344-respondent Indian survey found paper-only ahead of hybrid for every data type (intraoral findings 51% paper vs 34% hybrid), 47.5% fearing system crash/data loss and 35.5% citing assistants' reluctance to type (https://www.ovid.com/jnls/srmj/fulltext/10.4103/srmjrds.srmjrds_144_23~evaluating-the-knowledge-and-perception-of-electronic-health). So: tap, don't type; show local-plus-cloud redundancy on screen; be faster than a pen in week one or be abandoned.
3. **Offline-first, India-hosted.** 76.3% of 384 North Indian dental professionals named network access as the top teledentistry barrier (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12451562/). Local SQLite via Drift (https://pub.dev/packages/drift) synced by PowerSync (Pro from $49/month, 1,000 peak clients; https://powersync.com/pricing) to Supabase Postgres in Mumbai ap-south-1 (Pro $25/month; https://supabase.com/pricing). Isar, Realm Sync and ElectricSQL are dead or unsupported for Flutter (https://github.com/isar/isar).
4. **Cash, UPI and GST first; insurance is a plug-in.** Dentrix Ascend ran 191 million eligibility checks in 2025 (https://investor.henryschein.com/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Henry-Schein-One-Unveils-the-Next-Era-of-Dentrix-Ascend-for-DSOs-and-Growth-Focused-Practices/default.aspx); that entire core is dead weight in a market where dental OPD cover caps at Rs 1,500-25,000 with 1-2 year waits (https://www.tataaig.com/health-insurance/dental-insurance).
5. **WhatsApp is the channel, not a feature, but clinical content lives behind authenticated links.** 96.2% of 105 Pune GDPs used WhatsApp and 63.2% of second-opinion sharers obtained no consent (https://www.thejcdp.com/doi/10.5005/jp-journals-10024-2415). The Business Messaging Policy bars health-related content where regulation requires heightened protection (https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/), so send "your plan is ready" plus a link, never the plan.
6. **Compliance is product, not paperwork.** The DCI Code of Ethics 2014 (still operative after the NDC replaced the DCI on 19 March 2026; https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2242888&reg=48&lang=2) hard-codes required fields: registration number on every prescription, certificate and receipt, written estimates before treatment, 3-year retention and record copies within 72 hours (https://dciindia.gov.in/Rule_Regulation/Gazette_Notification_reg_DCI_Revised_Dentists_Code_of_Ethics_Regulations_2014_27.06.2014.pdf). Clinical records are append-only and never hard-deleted.
7. **One published flat price per clinic, no add-on ladder, no percentage of collections.** Position directly against Practo Ray's 1.8-2% payment fee on top of Rs 999-1,499/month (https://www.practo.com/providers/clinics/ray/plans) and against the US add-on ladder (Open Dental eServices bundle $165/month, AI image analysis $149/month; https://www.opendental.com/site/fees.html).
8. **Model once for the world.** FDI (ISO 3950) as the stored tooth key, SNOMED CT for findings, ICD-10 K00-K14 for diagnoses, FHIR R4 shaped like the NRCeS ABDM profiles (https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/index.html), DICOM for images. India is the MVP; US, UK and GCC editions become mappings, not rewrites.
9. **Small and opinionated.** Five dashboard numbers, not forty reports; the "31 features, I use 4" complaint is the Indian norm (anecdotal, https://codingclave.com/guides/best-dental-clinic-software-india-2026).
10. **Build the odontogram in-house; open-source only the geometry layer.** pub.dev has only tooth pickers (teeth_selector, dental_teeth_selector) with no surfaces, conditions or perio, and zero results for "odontogram" (https://pub.dev/packages/dental_teeth_selector). The MIT react-advanced-odontogram is the state-model reference (https://github.com/ZoliQua/React-Advanced-Odontogram). Decision (recommended): open-source only the tooth geometry and FDI/Universal/Palmer notation layer as a small `tooth_geometry` package; keep surfaces, conditions, perio, history and sync in the proprietary `chart_engine`, because the surface-level odontogram is the one thing competitors cannot pull from a package.

## MVP: first 90 days

Scope: one owner-dentist with 1-3 chairs, an assistant who doubles as receptionist (Indian setup guides price a receptionist at Rs 12,000-22,000/month and advise merging the role for the first 3-4 months; https://www.dentskart.com/blog/how-to-start-a-dental-practice-in-india-2026), and a visiting specialist paid 40-50% of the procedure fee (same source). One Flutter 3.47 codebase (Impeller default on Android API 29+ and iOS; https://docs.flutter.dev/release/whats-new) shipping Android and iOS; the front-desk web build is out of scope. Features are in build order; each must pass its acceptance test on a Rs 12,000-class Android phone offline.

1. **Patient identity and family.** Acceptance: new patient in 4 taps with name and mobile; duplicate detection by mobile; guardian record mandatory when DOB is under 18 (DPDP s.9 treats under-18 as a child; parental-consent duties commence 13 May 2027 but build now; https://dpdpa.dcomply.in/rules/); ABHA number and HPR/HFR ID fields present but optional; creates and reads offline, syncs within 30 seconds of reconnect; OTP by WhatsApp authentication template first (Rs 0.115; https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing), MSG91 SMS fallback at Rs 0.18-0.25 per OTP (https://msg91.com/in/pricing/otp) with DLT registration filed before launch, never Firebase phone auth at $0.07 per SMS to India (https://firebase.google.com/pricing).
2. **Appointment book.** Acceptance: chairs, providers and assistants are separate resources on a 10-minute grid; procedure templates carry assistant and doctor segments (crown 30/60/30 min per Burkhart's 3/6/3-unit model; https://www.burkhartdental.com/practice-guide/front-office-systems/constructing-zone-scheduling/); book, move and cancel in 3 taps; conflict detection; day view scrolls at 60 fps.
3. **WhatsApp confirmations and reminders.** Acceptance: approved utility templates for confirmation, 24-hour and morning-of reminders at Rs 0.115 per delivered message plus 18% GST (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing; INR figures per https://amomic.in/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india); "Reply 1 to confirm" writes the appointment status; per-patient channel preference and opt-in record; per-clinic message spend visible in settings; DLT-registered SMS fallback (entity registration Rs 5,900 all-in; https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/a-complete-guide-on-dlt-registration).
4. **Odontogram v1.** Acceptance: 32 permanent and 20 primary teeth, FDI stored, FDI/Universal/Palmer display toggle; tap tooth then tap surface(s) then pick condition or procedure; age-based default dentition with override; supernumerary entry; every state change appended to history, never overwritten; sub-16 ms frame on the reference phone.
5. **Procedure catalogue and treatment plan.** Acceptance: clinic-owned catalogue pre-seeded with roughly 120 Indian procedures and editable city-tier fee ranges (metro RCT Rs 3,500-7,000, smaller towns Rs 2,000-4,500; https://clovedental.in/blog/filling-root-canal/cost-of-root-canal-treatment-india); plan lines carry tooth, surfaces, procedure, fee, phase; Plan A vs Plan B alternatives; saving a plan freezes an immutable snapshot with a hash; acceptance recorded by stylus signature or OTP; funnel states presented, accepted, scheduled, completed.
6. **Procedure-specific consent with e-sign.** Acceptance: RCT, extraction, crown, implant, ortho, RPD/FPD/CD templates with {tooth_numbers} and fees merged; stylus signature, timestamp, device ID and template-version hash stored; guardian flow for minors; English, Hindi and Telugu at launch (Kannada, Malayalam and Tamil in months 7-9; Assamese only when an Assam or Arunachal clinic asks); sealed PDF generated on-device with the pdf and signature packages (https://pub.dev/packages/pdf) and shared as a link, not an attachment.
7. **Clinical note and photo capture.** Acceptance: BDS case-sheet structure (chief complaint, history, examination, diagnosis via ICD-10 K00-K14 picker, treatment, follow-up); every entry auto-signed, dated and timed (NABH DHSP IMS.3 expects entries within one hour; https://nabh-portal-live.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/13105241/Final-Dental-Standard-3rd-Edition.pdf, a single-checker source); camera capture auto-attached to tooth and visit with EXIF kept; append-only.
8. **Prescription.** Acceptance: drug master of about 40 dental molecules tagged H/H1/X with effective dates; PDF prints every Rule 65(10) and PCI field (see clinical spec); generic name in capitals with brand in brackets; allergy soft-block; H1 items flagged; verification QR.
9. **Estimate, invoice, receipt, ledger.** Acceptance: estimate issued before treatment and linked to the plan; invoice auto-built from completed procedures; per-line tax class; registration number on every receipt; partial payments, advances, typed discounts; family ledger; static UPI QR and upi:// intent (UPI Collect was deprecated 28 Feb 2026; https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/upi/); CSV export for the CA.
10. **Procedure-triggered recall.** Acceptance: completing scaling creates a 6-month recall, RCT creates a crown follow-up, configurable per procedure; recall list shows rupee value of overdue work; one-tap WhatsApp recall.
11. **Lab case tracking v1.** Acceptance: lab, teeth, type, material, shade, instructions; due date computed from the lab's turnaround skipping holidays (Open Dental pattern; https://www.opendental.com/manual/labcaseedit.html); statuses Created, Sent, Received, QC'd, Fitted; lab slip PDF to the lab's WhatsApp; remake flag; seat appointment cannot be confirmed until Received.
12. **Export and audit.** Acceptance: one-tap patient-record export (PDF plus images plus CSV) in under two minutes, satisfying the 72-hour duty; whole-clinic export; immutable audit table with user, action, time and reason.
13. **Stretch: sterilisation and licence log.** Only if 1-12 are stable: daily chemical and weekly biological indicator log (CDC monitoring cadence; https://www.cdc.gov/dental-infection-control/hcp/dental-ipc-faqs/sterilization-monitoring.html) and expiry reminders for CEA, BMW authorisation and AERB registration.

**Explicitly out of scope for the 90 days:** insurance claims, CDT codes and eligibility; ABDM production certification (sandbox work starts, exit lands after day 90); any AI radiograph analysis or voice perio charting; patient app or portal; public online-booking page; Flutter-web front desk; multi-branch; inventory; payroll and commission statements; DICOM/TWAIN sensor bridge; video teledentistry; membership plans; marketing broadcasts; accounting integrations; NHCX; US/UK/GCC localisation.

## Clinical module spec

### Tooth model and notation

Store one canonical tooth key and render the rest. FDI is what Indian dentists learn (the DCI BDS curriculum teaches ADA, Zsigmondy-Palmer and FDI; https://dciindia.gov.in/Rule_Regulation/Revised_BDS_Course_Regulation_2007.pdf), Universal is "commonly used only in the United States", and Palmer persists in UK practice (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_notation).

| System | Permanent | Primary | Supernumerary | Where it is the default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FDI / ISO 3950 (stored key) | 11-48 | 51-85 | none in the standard; product convention = base FDI + index | India, WHO, EU, GCC |
| Universal (ADA) | 1-32 | A-T | base + 50 (51-82), letter + S (AS-TS), as Open Dental stores them (https://www.opendental.com/OpenDentalDocumentation26-1.xml) | US claims and CDT |
| Palmer | quadrant glyph + 1-8 | A-E | none | UK, orthodontists |

Each tooth row in a static lookup carries FDI, Universal, Palmer, a SNOMED CT body-structure ID and a SNODENT ID (SNODENT is a no-cost ADA licence; the HL7 Dental IG's Tooth Identification set has 207 concepts; https://hl7.org/fhir/us/dental-data-exchange/ValueSet-tooth-identification-valueset.html). Surfaces are a fixed enum: M, D, O/I, B/F, L/P, plus V (class V cervical, collapsed into B/F on any export), ROOT and WHOLE. Treatment area mirrors Open Dental's enum: surface, tooth, quadrant, sextant, arch, tooth-range, mouth (https://www.opendental.com/Manual/entertreatment.html). Mixed dentition: each arch position is a slot that can hold a primary and a permanent tooth independently, each with an eruption state (unerupted, erupting, erupted, exfoliated, missing, retained); default dentition by DOB (primary to 6, mixed roughly 6.5-11, permanent 11+, third molars 12-19, per Carestream Sensei; https://help.carestreamdental.com/rh/web/server/Sensei_Cloud/projects_responsive/CM300_SCWorkbook/Charting_Mixed_Dentition.htm). Open Dental cannot perio-chart primary or supernumerary teeth and makes users mark unerupted molars as "primary" (https://www.opendental.com/manual243/missingteeth.html); do better, because paediatric dentistry is a large Indian specialty.

### Conditions and findings

A Condition is a surface-level record coded with SNOMED CT (free in India under the NRCeS affiliate licence; adopt the SNOMED International Odontogram and General Dentistry refsets, July 2026 editions; https://mlds.ihtsdotools.org/api/releasePackages/173263) and ICD-10 K00-K14 (https://icd.who.int/browse10/2019/en#/K00-K14), with ICDAS 0-6 severity for caries (https://www.iccms-web.com/content/icdas). Keep conditions separate from procedures (Open Dental's "Cn" status) and record existing work by other providers separately. Mobility 0-3 and furcation (stored 0-3, with a clinic-level display mapping to Glickman I-IV) live on the perio exam.

### Perio chart

Six sites per tooth (MB, B, DB, ML, L, DL) with one row per tooth per measurement type: probing depth, gingival margin (signed integers; avoid Open Dental's "100+ means negative" encoding), bleeding, suppuration, plaque, calculus, MGJ, furcation, and per-tooth mobility; skip-teeth and implants flagged (https://www.opendental.com/manual/perio.html). CAL = PD + gingival margin and whole-mouth bleeding percentage are derived; 2017 AAP/EFP stage and grade are clinician-entered because they need radiographic bone loss, tooth loss and risk factors the chart does not hold (https://www.perio.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Staging-and-Grading-Periodontitis.pdf). A BPE screening layer (codes 0-4 and *, WHO probe, six sextants) gates charting: code 3 unlocks a six-point chart for that sextant, code 4 unlocks full-mouth charting, and BPE is blocked around implants (https://www.bsperio.org.uk/assets/downloads/BSP_BPE_Guidelines_2019.pdf). Entry ergonomics decide adoption: fixed grid, two auto-advance sequences, undo, colour thresholds, an assistant "call-out" mode, and six exams overlaid (current plus five prior).

### Treatment plan

Copy Open Dental's three states, Active, Inactive and Saved (https://www.opendental.com/manual/treatmentplan.html), but replace the primary/secondary insurance columns with a cash patient-portion model. Phases are priorities with subtotals; alternatives are Plan A vs Plan B (RCT plus crown vs extraction plus implant). Saving freezes line descriptions, teeth and fees; acceptance binds a signature to the hash of note, date and every line's description and amount. Any later change creates a new version and re-triggers consent.

### Consent

The DCI Code contains the word "consent" zero times; the operative standard is Samira Kohli (real consent on nature, purpose, risks and alternatives; a relative cannot consent for a competent adult; https://indiankanoon.org/doc/438423/). Dental consumer fora punish the absent form: Sravani Dental (NCDRC 2022, Rs 5 lakh) recorded "Admittedly, the petitioner has not obtained written consent", and Sabka Dentist v. Kalpana Tripathi (Maharashtra SCDRC, Aug 2026, Rs 4.32 lakh at 12% plus Rs 2 lakh) turned on missing pre-operative IOPAs (single-checker summaries; https://indiankanoon.org/doc/82459637/). Mandatory fields: procedure template, tooth numbers, nature and purpose, material risks in plain language, alternatives including no treatment, estimate reference, signer identity and capacity (guardian for minors), dentist name and registration number, timestamp, signature or OTP, immutable audit trail. Recommended: a free-text "what was discussed" note, language of explanation, witness, and implied-consent templates for examination and scaling. A stylus signature is evidence of agreement under IT Act s.10A; Aadhaar eSign under s.3A is the only Second-Schedule signature and should be an optional upgrade for implants, full-mouth and ortho cases (https://www.leegality.com/blog/what-is-esign). Keep a second, separate consent object for DPDP purposes (treatment, billing, marketing, photo use) with withdrawal history; those duties commence 13 May 2027 (https://dpdpa.dcomply.in/rules/).

### Prescriptions and India e-Rx rules

A dentist is a "Registered medical practitioner" under Rule 2(ee)(iv) of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, so Rule 65(10) applies: the prescription must be in writing, signed with the usual signature, dated, carry the patient's name and address, and state total quantity and dose; Rule 65(11) means it is dispensed once unless marked repeatable (https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/acts_rules/2016DrugsandCosmeticsAct1940Rules1945.pdf). PCI Regulation 2(j) recognises "written or electronic" prescriptions from dentists and lists the contents pharmacists check: prescriber name, qualification, address, registration number, patient name, address, age, sex, reference number, date, Rx symbol, medication, directions, refill note, signature (https://www.pci.nic.in/pdf/Pharmacy%20Practice%20Regulations.pdf). No statute mandates a DSC; a signature image plus a server-side hash and verification QR is dispensable in practice, and Aadhaar eSign is an "ABDM-grade" option. Generic names in capitals (Code 3.5). The drug master carries a schedule tag with effective dates: pregabalin enters Schedule H1 around 16 Nov 2026 via GSR 377(E) (https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Notifications/Gazette-Notifications/), and all third-generation cephalosporins, respiratory fluoroquinolones, tramadol and benzodiazepines are already H1. Antibiotic stewardship matters: a 50-study Indian review found antibiotics in 27-88% of dental prescriptions (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8698453/). The 2020 Telemedicine Practice Guidelines bind only IMC-registered RMPs (https://www.nrces.in/download/files/pdf/Telemedicine.pdf); adopt them voluntarily: block Schedule X and NDPS drugs remotely, no first H1 prescription by video, consent and identity logged.

### Imaging and photo capture

The phone camera is the imaging device for most 1-3 chair clinics; CBCT at Rs 20-60 lakh is "not required" for general dentistry and RVG sensors run Rs 2-5 lakh (https://ichelonconsulting.com/insights/dental-clinic-cost-india-2026-complete-breakdown). Ship guided intraoral views, before/after pairing, auto-attach to tooth and visit, compression with EXIF retained (https://pub.dev/packages/flutter_image_compress), originals in object storage. Store your own tooth tags (FDI list, view type BW/PA/OPG/CBCT/photo, FMX slot) because DICOM still has no standard way to say which teeth are in an intraoral image (https://wiki.ihe.net/index.php/Display_Mapping_and_Viewing_Solution_for_Intraoral_Radiography). Sensors are captured through Windows TWAIN drivers (https://opendental.com/manual/imagingsetup.html is the reference pattern; Open Dental supports only 32-bit TWAIN), so a thin Windows capture-bridge agent is a v2 item; the pure-Dart dicom_viewer decodes JPEG baseline and lossless but not JPEG 2000 (https://pub.dev/packages/dicom_viewer). Every share of a photo logs consent, with a watermark and identity masking option.

### Lab case tracking

Fields: lab, case type (crown, bridge, denture, aligner, night guard), teeth, material, shade (VITA classical or 3D-Master), impression or scan type, instructions, fee, due date computed from the lab's per-service turnaround skipping holidays, timestamps Created, Sent, Received, Quality-Checked, Fitted, remake flag with reason, lab cost attached to the procedure for margin (https://www.opendental.com/manual/labcaseedit.html). Remake-rate target under 3% is a US rule of thumb (https://resources.rework.com/libraries/dental-clinic-growth/dental-lab-cost-management); Indian labs are unbenchmarked, so each clinic enters its own price lists.

### Implant, ortho and endo tracking

Implants: scan the GS1 or HIBC pack barcode to capture brand, system, diameter and length, lot, UDI and expiry while the pack is still in the room; record site, insertion torque, graft and membrane lots; generate a patient implant passport (EU MDR Article 18 implant cards apply from 26 May 2021 and the Council of European Dentists says dental implants are not exempt; https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/aefcab91-11e1-4532-abb7-3d88e221b998_en); explant and failure states for recalls. Ortho: a structured case object (appliance, bracket prescription and slot, archwire sequence, elastics, aligner stage N of M with change interval and projected finish, compliance events, debond date, retention protocol) plus per-visit photos, which leaps past the date-by-column grids of Open Dental and OrthoTrac (https://www.opendental.com/manual/orthochart.html). Endo: per-canal rows (MB, MB2, DB, P and so on) with reference point, working length by apex locator and radiograph, initial and master apical file, irrigation, obturation technique, sealer and length grade, iatrogenic events, and multi-visit status; a pre-operative IOPA is required before access, or a documented reason, because its absence is what fora call negligence.

### Medical-history red flags

A structured questionnaire that emits stored risk objects and procedure-level banners, not a free-text box. Anticoagulants: warfarin needs an INR within 72 hours, proceed below 4 with local haemostasis; DOACs follow SDCEP's miss-or-delay rules for higher-bleeding-risk procedures and are never stopped (https://www.sdcep.org.uk/published-guidance/anticoagulants-and-antiplatelets/). Anti-resorptives and anti-angiogenics: assign and record a low or higher MRONJ risk group, show a banner on extraction, implant and perio surgery, and schedule an 8-week socket review (https://www.sdcep.org.uk/published-guidance/medication-related-osteonecrosis-of-the-jaw/; incidence figures are disputed between checkers and deliberately omitted). Also structured: diabetes with HbA1c, pregnancy trimester, penicillin and local-anaesthetic allergies, cardiac conditions needing prophylaxis, bleeding disorders, steroids, epilepsy, asthma. SDCEP is Scottish NHS guidance with no NDC equivalent; label it as an international reference and keep the rule table data-driven.

## Front office, billing and CRM spec

### Multi-chair scheduling

Chair, provider and assistant are separate resources so a doctor can run two columns while assistant-only segments overlap (crown prep 3/6/3 ten-minute units, limited exam 1/1/1, endo 1/7/3; https://www.burkhartdental.com/practice-guide/front-office-systems/constructing-zone-scheduling/). Block templates (new patient, large case, emergency) release automatically after 3 days; an ASAP list backfills cancellations. India-specific: recall columns belong to the dentist, not a hygienist, because registered hygienists are scarce (unverified headcount), and visiting-specialist days carry their own fee-share rule. Everything must be doable one-handed between patients, because the dentist is the receptionist for the first year.

### Reminders and recall cadence

| Touch | Timing | Category and cost | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation | immediate | Utility, Rs 0.115 (https://amomic.in/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) | table stakes |
| Reminder 1 | 7 days before | Utility | timing (day vs week) made no clear difference in a 29-study review (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=PMCID:PMC3188816&resultType=core&format=json) |
| Reminder 2, "Reply 1 to confirm" | 24-48 h | Utility; reply is free inside the 24-hour window | automated reminders cut no-shows 22.95% across 1.6M appointments in a vendor pre/post study (https://us.dental-tribune.com/news/study-reveals-how-automated-patient-appointment-reminders-affect-dental-practice-no-show-rates-and-production/) |
| Morning-of | same day | Utility | vendor guidance, untested in India |
| Unconfirmed high-value slot | 24 h | human call | manual calls beat automation, roughly 39% vs 29% relative reduction (same review); Parikh 2010 saw 23.1% no-show fall to 17.3% automated and 13.6% with staff calls (https://www.dialoghealth.com/post/patient-appointment-reminder-statistics) |
| Recall | per procedure, NICE menu 3-24 months (https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/CG19/chapter/recommendations) | Marketing if promotional, Rs 0.8631; keep it a care-plan utility message where the patient agreed the interval | US practices average 65-70% recall completion with 85-90% achievable (vendor whitepaper, https://clerri.com/blog/dental-patient-reactivation-statistics) |
| Reactivation | lapse bands 6-12, 12-18, 18-24, 24+ months, 3-5 touches | Marketing, opt-in required | vendor figures only; instrument your own Indian baseline |

Expect 20-25% fewer no-shows, not the 40-70% Indian vendor testimonials claim; the only measured Indian figure is paediatric (52% ever-missed, Bhatia 2018) and one clinic reported 40% no-shows on marketplace bookings, so instrument your own baseline (https://smartdentaldesk.com/blog/whatsapp-marketing-for-dental-clinics).

### Estimates, invoices and the ledger

Three documents, one chain. The estimate is issued before treatment and versioned with a justification on any increase (Code 5.7.1). The invoice is built from completed procedures, carries SAC or HSN per line, and is titled "Invoice-cum-Bill of Supply" for a registered clinic billing an unregistered patient under Rule 46A (https://taxhandout.com/gst-on-dentists-dental-clinics/), or a plain receipt for the unregistered majority. Every receipt prints the registration number (Code 3.4.1). The ledger is a true family account: charges, payments by method (cash, UPI, card, EMI, bank), typed adjustments (discount, write-off, refund), advances and deposits, patient-only aging in 0-30/31-60/61-90/90+ buckets (https://www.opendental.com/manual/reportaging.html), and a visiting-specialist share computed per procedure net of lab fees. Export a Form 3C-style daily case register by financial year (Income-tax Rule 6F, six years; https://indiankanoon.org/doc/56057029/, single-checker).

### GST handling

| Line class | Treatment | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Therapeutic services (SAC 999312) | Exempt | Notification 12/2017 entry 74; Maharashtra AAR held crowns, bridges and restoratives exempt (https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/no-gst-on-providing-dental-health-care-services-by-dental-clinic-aar/) |
| Crowns, implants, aligners supplied inside treatment | Exempt as composite supply (medium confidence) | same |
| Purely cosmetic (bleaching, smile-design veneers) | Taxable: 5% without ITC under group 99972 since 22 Sep 2025, or 18% with ITC; [disputed] between checkers, default 18% and make it configurable | https://www.gstindiaonline.com/pages/gstlegislation/GST-Nfy/Central%20Tax%20Rate/2025/15-2025-CTR.pdf |
| Over-the-counter goods (HSN 9021, 3306) | 5% | 56th Council FAQ Q24 (https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/faq.pdf) |

Registration is triggered only when the clinic makes a taxable supply and aggregate turnover (which includes exempt supplies) crosses Rs 20 lakh for services (https://cleartax.in/s/gst-doctors-medical-fees); one checker put Arunachal Pradesh at Rs 10 lakh, two put it at Rs 20 lakh, so make the threshold a state table. Keep a rate-history table and a GSTR-1/3B export that reports exempt turnover.

### UPI, Razorpay and financing

Never touch funds: RBI's 2025 Payment Aggregator Master Direction requires Rs 15 crore net worth, escrow and settlement to the merchant's account only (https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896). In-clinic collection uses the clinic's own static UPI QR or upi:// intent at zero cost; remote collection uses Razorpay payment links at 2% plus GST (UPI QR 0.99%; https://razorpay.com/pricing/) or Cashfree at 1.95% (https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-charges/). Model the clinic as a sub-merchant later (Razorpay Route 0.1% per split, gated on Rs 40 lakh turnover; Cashfree Easy Split 0.2%). From 15 Oct 2026, unless deferred, UPI P2M above Rs 2,000 carries 0.4% MDR capped at Rs 300 plus GST (https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-upi-mdr-above-rs-2000-october-15/), squarely on dental tickets, so tag each UPI payment with MDR applicability behind a feature flag. Memberships ride UPI AutoPay (AFA-free up to Rs 15,000 per debit under the 2026 e-mandate framework; https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13374) and are MDR-exempt. EMI: surface Bajaj subvention of 6% (3 months) to 13.5% (12 months) via Razorpay on the quote so dentists price implants knowingly (https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/emi/). Store only gateway tokens, last four digits and brand (RBI's card-on-file tokenisation framework (circular RBI/2021-22/96 of 7 Sep 2021, deadline extended to 1 Oct 2022 by RBI/2022-23/77; https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx)).
